A free computer tool that helps communities design safer emergency shelters

OpenShelter combines public hazard data with real building codes to produce plain-language design briefs — every recommendation traceable to the rule that produced it.

Generate a brief ↓ View on GitHub
1 · Where?You enter a location (latitude & longitude) and who it must protect.
2 · Which rules?Pick the local building code — or use our ready-made profiles for India, EU, US, Philippines & Sphere standards.
3 · Get the briefIn seconds you get a printable checklist: structure, water, heat, sanitation — each item citing its source.

Live demo — runs entirely in your browser, nothing uploaded

⚠️ Demo uses a synthetic hazard field for illustration. It produces pre-design guidance, not stamped engineering — a licensed local engineer must verify structural calculations before construction.
