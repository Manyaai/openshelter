The problem in one paragraph

Safe knowledge exists. It just isn't connected.

Billions of dollars flow into emergency shelters every year. Most are designed from copy-paste templates that ignore local codes and local hazards, so the same "safe" shelter that survives an earthquake in one country collapses in a flood somewhere else. Hazard maps exist. Building codes exist. Nothing free and auditable connected them, until now.
