Generate a shelter design brief, right in your browser

Pick a famous high-risk site or type any coordinates. The tool reads real earthquake and cyclone data, applies the building code you choose, and prints a plain-language brief where every recommendation cites its source. Nothing is uploaded; this runs entirely on your device.

Honesty policy. Earthquake & cyclone scores come from real public-domain data (USGS ComCat M6.0+ 1990-2026; NOAA IBTrACS climatology). Flood, heat, drought and landslide values are labelled demo placeholders until national agencies contribute authoritative layers. OpenShelter produces pre-design guidance, not stamped engineering, a licensed local engineer must verify structural calculations before construction.
